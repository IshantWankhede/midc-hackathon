Demystifying The Event

Wait... What IS a Hackathon? 🤔

A hackathon is basically an exciting mini-startup challenge where teams come together to solve real-world problems for big cash prizes and government contracts!

💡

1. It Starts with a Real Problem

MIDC (Maharashtra Industrial Development Corporation) manages thousands of factories. They have real problems like broken roads, chemical waste, carbon smoke, and gas leaks.

🧩

2. We Combine Our Strengths

The tech team builds the software/prototype, while you design the pitch deck, figure out the business model, and explain why it saves MIDC money!

🏆

3. We Pitch & Win Pilots!

We present a short 5-minute pitch to a panel of expert judges. Winning teams get cash prizes out of ₹2 Crore and government work orders!

🚫 Busting Common Hackathon Myths

❌ Myth: "I need to know Python or Coding." ✅ Fact: 40% of judging criteria is based on business viability, slide deck storytelling, and operational impact!
❌ Myth: "It takes months of hard work." ✅ Fact: Hackathon proposals are short 5-minute slide decks and quick visual demos!
❌ Myth: "Is the ₹2 Crore prize real?" ✅ Fact: Yes! Supported directly by the Maharashtra State Government (MIDC).
