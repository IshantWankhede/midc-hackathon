Official Proposal Submission Answers

Key extracts from our submitted application form:

⚠️

1. What is the Problem?

Industrial estates like MIDC carry continuous 24/7 heavy container traffic (40-tonne trucks) under heavy monsoons. Road inspection relies on slow manual reporting, leaving potholes unaddressed for weeks, leading to expensive breakdowns and severe traffic jams.

🎯

2. Solution Summary (3 Steps)

1. Automated Spotting: Small cameras on buses/trucks scan roads daily.
2. Smart Estimations & Scheduling: Auto-calculates repair costs, materials & schedules night fixes.
3. Fast & Permanent Repairs: Dries in 30 mins for 40-tonne trucks.

🚀

3. What is Innovative?

Instead of buying expensive specialized vehicles, we mount smart cameras on existing municipal fleets for daily road scanning. We auto-calculate exact repair costs, material volumes, and fix timelines before workers leave the warehouse.
