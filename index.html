Non-Tech Jargon Buster

      
Quick definitions of hackathon terms so you sound like an industry expert!

    


    

      

        
TRL 4+ (Technology Readiness Level)

        
Means your solution is past theoretical idea phase and has a working lab/pilot prototype validated in real conditions.

      

      

        
Effluent & CETP

        
Effluent is industrial liquid waste. CETP stands for Common Effluent Treatment Plant where waste water from multiple factories is cleaned together.

      

      

        
ZLD (Zero Liquid Discharge)

        
A wastewater treatment process where all water is purified and recycled back into the factory, leaving zero liquid waste to pollute drains.

      

      

        
P2P Solar Microgrid

        
A localized power network allowing neighboring factories to sell excess solar energy directly to each other without relying solely on the state grid.

      

      

        
Edge AI & Vision AI

        
Running artificial intelligence models directly on local cameras or hardware boxes (on the edge) without sending video data to slow cloud servers.

      

      

        
3D Digital Twin

        
A virtual 3D computer model of physical factories, pipelines, and roads that updates in real-time using live sensor data.
